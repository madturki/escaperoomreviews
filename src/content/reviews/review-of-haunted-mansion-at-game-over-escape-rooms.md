## What others are saying

A classic haunted-house escape room with sharply divided reviews: atmosphere wins praise, puzzles less so.

Google reviewers call it genuinely scary, with big, well-thought-out rooms and good clues. Reviewers on Morty enjoy the haunted-house ambience, jump scares and friendly hosts.

Morty reviewers are critical of the puzzle design: several describe repetitive puzzles and solutions that rely on trial and error without clear cluing, and say the narrow hallways are awkward for large groups. One group finished very quickly and found little challenge, and another complained about the game master's attitude.
