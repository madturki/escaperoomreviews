## What others are saying

This room now runs as "Final Destination 2.0" at Roundabout. Reviews are generally positive about its airplane set, but more lukewarm about the game as a whole, especially in recent years.

The airplane cabin is the clear highlight. Back in 2015, the Escape Games Review blog said it felt like a real plane, right down to the seats and overhead compartments. The We Try to Escape blog called it one of the best airplane cabins they'd seen and singled out one clever, unique puzzle inside it. One player on the Morty app described the puzzles as logical and fair, and praised the customer service. It's not scary, and We Try to Escape called it a family-friendly experience.

The common complaints are its size and age. Reviewers agree it's small and linear, and best with only two or three players. We Try to Escape found most puzzles fairly easy and one-step, thought the last space was very basic, and weren't sure how the first space tied into the story. Recent Morty reviews say the room is showing its age and could use a refresh. One player said some puzzles were "janky" and didn't trigger even when solved, and felt the game was on the short side for the price.
