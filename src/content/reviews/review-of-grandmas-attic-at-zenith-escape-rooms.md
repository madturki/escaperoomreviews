## What others are saying

Zenith Escape Rooms appears to have closed permanently, so Grandma's Attic can no longer be played. Morty lists the Bayview Drive location as no longer operational, and federal corporate records show the company was dissolved in December 2025.

We couldn't find any reviews of Grandma's Attic specifically. As for the company in general, Barrie locals on Reddit spoke well of it, with comments like "Zenith is great" and "pretty good." Review aggregator Yably shows it held consistently strong ratings on Google.

The only real caution we came across was second-hand: one Reddit commenter said they'd heard a couple of Zenith's rooms were "crazy hard" and the experience overall was pretty intense.
