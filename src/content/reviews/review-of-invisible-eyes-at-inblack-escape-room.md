## What others are saying

Invisible Eyes is a short, beginner-oriented mystery room at INBLACK's Eaton location, and the reception is mostly negative.

On the plus side, reviewers on Morty say it is easy and suits first-timers, with a haunted-house feel, and one Google reviewer recommended it for beginners.

Most complaints are about the lack of gameplay. Google reviewers describe essentially one puzzle, much of the 30 minutes spent watching AI-generated video and voiceover, heavy flashing lights and a puzzle that was broken. Morty reviewers similarly mention only one or two puzzles, flashing lights and a room in need of repair.
