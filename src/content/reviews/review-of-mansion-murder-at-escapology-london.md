## What others are saying

A multi-room Scottish-mansion whodunit that reviewers agree is one of Escapology London's trickier rooms.

Reviewers on Morty describe nice theming, a recognisable Scottish mansion and fun puzzles with plenty of tech. The We Try To Escape blog enjoyed the believable murder-mystery story, clues well integrated into the décor and a challenging variety of puzzles spread across several rooms, and Google reviewers call it difficult but fun.

The blog found the décor less realistic than it could have been, such as wallpaper standing in for library shelves, and notes the room is linear and may be too hard for younger kids. A Morty reviewer was confused by some elements, including the ending, and points out that the puzzles rely heavily on sight and colour vision.
