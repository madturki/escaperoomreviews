_The arch-criminal Matador has stolen the blueprint to the Grand Central Bank. He is threatening to contaminate all the gold bullion and crash the stock market if the city does not pay a ransom of $25,000,000 within the next hour. He has hidden the blueprints in one of the city’s streets, so his plan proceeds even if he is captured._

_You must use your superpowers to recover the blueprint and prevent his minions from carrying out the plan. Use your keen senses to find and use gadgets to help you on your quest to save the gold and the city!_

## What others are saying

Escape the Mystery Room at the Walden Galleria (later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) appears to have closed for good. TripAdvisor reports the location as permanently closed and Morty says the venue is no longer operating. All In Adventures still offers a tabletop Superhero's Adventure game through its Buffalo-area Mobile Mystery program, but that is a portable event format rather than the original room.

We couldn't find any reviews of Superhero's Adventure specifically, so here's what people said about the venue in general. TripAdvisor reviewers often praised the friendly, helpful staff, found the puzzles a good challenge and liked that the rooms skipped the horror.

Critics weren't as kind. Some TripAdvisor reviewers described small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers of combination locks, unclear instructions, hard-to-read clues and locks that didn't work properly, and an older player found everything was set so low they basically had to sit on the floor. On Reddit, an experienced Buffalo player advised skipping the venue, calling it too expensive for what you got.
