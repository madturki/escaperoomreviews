We have not yet completed a review of Enchanted Circus at Escape OK in Richmond Hill. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

An easy, family-friendly circus room for small groups that reviewers enjoy, especially with kids.

Reviewers on Morty call it cute, with lots of physical play and searching, and a good first escape room for children. Google reviewers mention unexpected puzzles that use senses you don't usually use in escape rooms, a well-designed room, and a mixed group of adults and kids having fun.

Morty reviewers say it is very easy and not tech-heavy, and that some spaces could be more visually interesting. One group found a door left open, which staff fixed right away.
