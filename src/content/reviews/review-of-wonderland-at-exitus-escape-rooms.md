_You have fallen down the rabbit hole and landed somewhere between a dream and a nightmare. Find your way to the Mad Hatter's tea party, where nothing makes sense and everything is a clue. Navigate the chaos, think sideways, and whatever you do, do not attract the attention of the Queen of Hearts._

We have not yet completed a review of Wonderland at Exitus Escape Rooms in Guelph. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Wonderland is Exitus's Alice-themed room. Families in particular have reviewed it warmly.

A Tripadvisor reviewer whose family of five played at intermediate level loved the collection of cryptograms, riddles and contraptions, said the room uses its whole space, and found several puzzles new to them. Google reviewers who played with young children call the puzzles interesting, say it isn't scary at all, and praise the staff for well-judged hints and a good mix of easier and harder puzzles. A reviewer on Morty says the staff were great with kids.

We found no notable complaints about this room in the reviews we read.
