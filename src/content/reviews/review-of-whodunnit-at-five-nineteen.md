A nona murdered because of her family recipe? What is this world coming to? We just had to solve this riddle.

Five Nineteen is the rebrand of the former Break Out Escapes in Cambridge, Ontario. The new owners have refreshed the space and the rooms. So, if it's been a while since you've visited, it's a great time to go back.

This room was well decorated like you'd expect a grandma's house to be. An old table, hutch, and vintage TV and place settings of grandma's finest china. It feels like a few pieces were missing. One, the music was good but I'd consider something from an older era. I'd add a large round carpet under the table to make it cozier. I know ceilings and lighting are hard in escape rooms but killing the fluorescent lights and lighting the room with lamps would help complete the feel. Finally, a couple of moth balls hidden in corners would round out the sensory immersion.

The puzzles were clever and all made sense and there were a couple that felt more rare than others. Did they fit the theme? Well, it's hard to imagine what grandma may come up with to hide a super secret good recipe. So, some of the puzzles didn't really fit the theme but they were all good. You're provided with boogie boards to keep notes and a calculator "just in case."

The staff member helping us was great. She was very happy and friendly which helps people have a good time. We didn't need the hint system but there is a hint book available inside the room. We completed the room in about 45 minutes with the two of us. It's a medium difficulty with minimal technology. It's large enough for 4 people but if you get beyond 5 it may start to get tight.

Although this wasn't one of the fanciest rooms we've done, it was actually quite fun and we'd do it again. I think with a few more immersive touches and maybe one or two puzzles reworded for nona, this room would get a great rating.

If you're in Cambridge, check it out!
