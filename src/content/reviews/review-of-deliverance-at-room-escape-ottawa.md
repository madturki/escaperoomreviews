We have not yet completed a review of Deliverance at Room Escape Ottawa in Ottawa. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Deliverance starts as a simple late-night pizza delivery and grows into something stranger across a large, multi-level space. Reviewers on Morty and the We Try To Escape blog both praise its original storyline.

Reviewers on Morty love walking into the multi-level set and the creative opening, and several call the first room the star of the show. We Try To Escape counts it among their favourites for its unique scenario, realistic alley set, huge rooms, wide variety of puzzles and surprising finale. They recommend it for groups of four to six.

Several Morty reviewers felt the game loses steam in its final puzzles. They also mention worn set pieces from heavy traffic and a few finicky props in the second half, and one found too many locks and some plot holes. We Try To Escape was disappointed when a hint request got the answer instead, and the game involves stairs.
