## What others are saying

Coma is a two-room, story-driven game set inside a patient's mind. It's one of Exitus's harder rooms, and reviews are mostly positive, with mixed feelings about its linear flow.

Reviewers on Morty enjoy the immersive story, the atmospheric second room, some fun tech puzzles and the attentive game masters, and several like being able to pick a difficulty level. A Tripadvisor reviewer found the intermediate level just right for four adults. Google reviewers describe a slow, hint-heavy first half that gives way to some really cool puzzles near the end.

The most common complaint is linearity. A Google reviewer who played on expert called it "extremely linear" and not suited to experienced players or large groups, and Morty players mention the puzzle-lock-puzzle-lock pattern. Morty reviewers also found the start of the second room confusing, and some had trouble with props that locked again or audio clues that triggered too early. Two Morty players advise against hard mode, and a Google reviewer felt it was not one of the most elaborate rooms they have played.
