_It's now post Cold War, but you and your fellow foreign agents have infiltrated a safehouse that has been used by a Secret agent. Objects, some from the past and some more current, seem to paint a picture of occupation. You will need to use all your wits and counter-espionage skills if you are to discover the secret missile codes and save civilisation as we know it._

We have not yet completed a review of Secret Agent at Saga Escapes in Mississauga. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A short, old-school spy room set in a Cold War safehouse, with mixed reception. The We Try To Escape blog reviewed it under its earlier name, KGB Safehouse.

Reviewers on Morty like that it is noticeably harder than the venue's other rooms, and one called it a really good room.

Morty players felt rushed by the 45-minute limit and found all of Saga's rooms similar in feel. In an older review, We Try To Escape was much harsher: they criticized a tiny, cramped set that did not match the story, puzzles they found boring, poor flow, and hints delivered by the game master walking into the room.
