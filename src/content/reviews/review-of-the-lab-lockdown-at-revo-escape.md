We have not yet completed a review of The Lab: Lockdown at Revo Escape in Toronto. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

The Lab: Lockdown is a physical, prison-break-style game built around improvising with whatever you find in the set rather than solving traditional puzzles. Reviewers on Morty are very positive about it, and it has also been reviewed by Room Escape Artist and the We Try To Escape blog.

Reviewers on Morty describe MacGyver-style challenges where you build solutions out of the props around you. Room Escape Artist found the ahas "exceptionally satisfying", enjoyed the funny intro video and a story with more depth than expected, and liked the post-game video showing how other teams solved things. We Try To Escape called it very immersive and suggested it works best with about four players.

On the downside, Room Escape Artist spent 40 minutes on the first challenge while only two teammates could take part, struggled to tell what was in play, and felt the handcuffs added little beyond discomfort and bruises. Morty reviewers also mention bumps and bruises, along with some visual clutter and mannequins that look unrealistic.
