## What others are saying

Escape the Mystery Room at the Walden Galleria (later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) appears to have closed for good. TripAdvisor reports the location as permanently closed and Morty says the venue is no longer operating, so the Special Agent room can no longer be played here. All In Adventures does still offer a tabletop Special Agent game through its Buffalo-area Mobile Mystery program, but that is a portable event format with its own jewel-thief storyline.

We couldn't find any reviews of this room specifically, so here's what people said about the venue in general. TripAdvisor reviewers often praised the friendly, helpful staff, enjoyed the challenge and recommended it as a fun outing with friends that skips the horror.

On the downside, critics described small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers of combination locks, instructions that weren't explained well, clues that were hard to read and locks that wouldn't open even with the right code. On Reddit, an experienced Buffalo player recommended skipping the venue, calling it too expensive for "a little office" with poorly ordered puzzles.
