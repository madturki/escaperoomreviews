## What others are saying

Escape From Alcatraz appears to be gone along with its venue. World of Escapes lists the room as closed, TripAdvisor reports the Walden Galleria location (later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) as permanently closed, and Morty says the venue is no longer operating. All In Adventures still offers a tabletop version of Escape From Alcatraz through its Buffalo-area Mobile Mystery program, but that is a portable event format rather than the original room.

We couldn't find any reviews of this room specifically, so here's what people said about the venue in general. TripAdvisor reviewers often praised the friendly, helpful staff, found the puzzles challenging and liked that the rooms had no horror elements.

The criticisms were fairly consistent: small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers of combination locks, instructions that weren't explained clearly, clues that were hard to read, and locks that sometimes wouldn't open with the right code. On Reddit, an experienced Buffalo player recommended skipping the venue, calling it too expensive for "a little office" with poorly ordered puzzles.
