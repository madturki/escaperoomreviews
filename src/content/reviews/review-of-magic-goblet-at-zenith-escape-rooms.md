"_Once upon a time, long, long ago, a competition was held between the bravest members of the magical world. This competition consisted of three most complex stages - the stage of dragons, the riddles of the underwater world, and the endless labyrinths. The champions who reached the end and set the magic goblet in the center of the playing field were promised eternal glory! However, the competition did not go as it was expected to. Once all the stages were completed, the cup, along with all the participants, disappeared from this world. You are now tasked with plunging into the memories of these brave souls, venturing through all three stages together with the champions. Your mission: to regain the magic goblet, and to finish what these brave souls had started._"

We have not yet completed a review of Magic Goblet at Zenith Escape Rooms in Barrie. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Zenith Escape Rooms appears to have closed permanently, so Magic Goblet can no longer be played. Morty lists the Bayview Drive location as no longer operational, and federal corporate records show the company was dissolved in December 2025.

We couldn't find any reviews of Magic Goblet specifically. As for the company in general, Barrie locals on Reddit spoke well of it, with comments like "Zenith is great" and "pretty good." Review aggregator Yably shows it held consistently strong ratings on Google.

The only real caution we came across was second-hand: one Reddit commenter said they'd heard a couple of Zenith's rooms were "crazy hard" and the experience overall was pretty intense.
