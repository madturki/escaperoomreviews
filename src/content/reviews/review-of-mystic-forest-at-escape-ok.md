## What others are saying

A whimsical, beginner-friendly fairy-forest room.

Reviewers on Morty love the first room, including one very unique puzzle, the nice set design and the friendly staff. Google reviewers call it creative and fun without being overly complicated, and praise the hosts for clear explanations and helpful clues.

Morty reviewers say it is very easy, so experienced players finish quickly, and that the later rooms don't live up to the first. They also mention a lack of direction and advise keeping groups small.
