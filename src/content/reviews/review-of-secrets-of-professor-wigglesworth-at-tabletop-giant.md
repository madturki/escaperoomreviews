## What others are saying

Secrets of Professor Wigglesworth is a family-friendly wizard-school mystery, the only escape room at Goderich's board game café. Players hunt for a missing pet that the headmaster may have stolen. There are only a handful of reviews: local families are enthusiastic, while a visiting enthusiast was more lukewarm.

Google reviewers describe it as well thought out and more challenging than they expected, and several have booked it for children's birthday parties. They praise hosts who ran the whole party and made it easy for the parents, and they mention a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

On Morty, an experienced player found the room to be mostly lock boxes. They felt the game master was too hand-holding, offering hints after only a few minutes on a puzzle, and said it wasn't memorable for someone passing through. They suggested it suits locals looking to try escape rooms more than enthusiasts. One Google reviewer also noted that plenty of clues were given to a room full of young kids.
