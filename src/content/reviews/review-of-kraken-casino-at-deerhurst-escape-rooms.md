## What others are saying

Kraken Casino is a steampunk-themed cabin at Deerhurst Resort where teams play casino-style games such as roulette and blackjack to shut down a dangerous power source. It plays differently from the venue's more traditional puzzle cabins.

A Tripadvisor reviewer described it as more of a timed skill-testing experience than a classic escape room, so it may appeal to groups who like playing games against the clock rather than working through a chain of locks.

The main complaint comes from a Google reviewer whose game was derailed by a mechanism that didn't work, leaving the group unable to progress; the venue offered a refund or discount afterwards. Players expecting a conventional run of logic puzzles may prefer one of the other cabins.
