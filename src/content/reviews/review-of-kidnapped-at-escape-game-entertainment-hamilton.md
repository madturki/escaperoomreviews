## What others are saying

A captivity-themed room that has drawn some of the harshest feedback at this venue, though a few groups had a great time.

On the positive side, Google reviewers describe a large group having a blast with a friendly host who helped whenever needed, and others found the puzzles logical and enjoyable to solve.

The complaints are hard to ignore. A reviewer on Morty called it one of the worst rooms they had played, pointing to a single raw space with pencil markings left on the walls, uncreative puzzles, an underdeveloped hint system and an unenthusiastic game master. Google reviewers say it offered little challenge, with one group of three escaping in under 20 minutes, felt low budget, and is set in the building's loading dock on a sloped concrete floor; one group also waited more than 10 minutes for staff after the ending video played.
