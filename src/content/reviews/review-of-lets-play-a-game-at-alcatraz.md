Please note that this room is rated as 18+ and includes some staff involvement.  It’s considered a horror room and earned a place on our list of scariest rooms in Ontario.

The environment is very well created.  It’s edgy, creepy, and gross.  There is a lot of attention to detail but the room does show some signs of wear that breaks the immersion.

The storyline is well presented and the theme is carried throughout the room and the puzzles. There's a great ambience thanks to the lighting and plastic sheeting that you can't _quite_ see through.

This is a medium-hard room with an assortment of puzzle types.  The puzzles are challenging but all very doable even a small group of two (though the room could easily accommodate four to six players.

We’d love to see rooms like this involving the staff more than the typical “boo!” jump scare.  While I understand that they’re not actors, some words, or actions like dragging a weapon or coming into the room to place or take an item would really take it up a notch.
