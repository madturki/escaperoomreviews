## What others are saying

A puzzle-dense Wizard of Oz adventure that players describe as challenging and fun.

Tripadvisor and Google reviewers say it gets teams working together, with creative puzzles and sweet, patient game masters, and Google reviewers found that the choice of difficulty level worked well for families and for groups mixing newcomers and veterans.

It is not an easy room: one Google group of four finished just a few minutes over time.
