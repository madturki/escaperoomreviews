The first thing that you need to know about Imaginarium is that it's designed and built by people who were in the film industry. The rooms are built like sets for a show or a movie. So, if you're looking for an incredibly immersive experience, this is it. As soon as the door is closed (or even before) you are standing in a convincing early 1900s hotel with the perfect finishing touches.

This is the perfect example of a 360 degree immersive room with walls, floors, ceilings, lighting, and audio cues all being exactly right.

It's also important to note right away that this room isn't scary. It's haunted, but whimsical. There is the theme of ghosts and supernatural events but there are no jump scares or dark moments.

The puzzles are well thought out and the progression through the room is logical. There is a great balance between physical locks, magical electronic triggers, and searching. We found some elements a bit repetitive and a couple of hints within the room unnecessary but they didn't materially subtract from the experience.

This is a medium difficulty room in a pretty big space. While we were able to complete it with two people, it's large enough to handle six people comfortably and up to eight people.

The staff here are fantastic with really great energy. The building is odd and it is a bit daunting to see this building that has more doors on it than KW Escapes "Exits" room. They do have a helpful video pinned on their Instagram account so you may want to check that out. They no longer have a pool table.
