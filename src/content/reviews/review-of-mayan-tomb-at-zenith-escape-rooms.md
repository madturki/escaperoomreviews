But you really shouldn’t have opened this tomb… You turn around to find that an ancient seal has already blocked your exit. Then, you hear a noise. A faint raspy breathing. We have not yet completed a review of Mayan Tomb at Zenith Escape Rooms in Barrie. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Zenith Escape Rooms appears to have closed permanently, so Mayan Tomb can no longer be played. Morty lists the Bayview Drive location as no longer operational, and federal corporate records show the company was dissolved in December 2025.

We couldn't find any reviews of Zenith's Mayan Tomb specifically. Several other companies have run rooms with the same name, so be careful not to mix them up if you go searching. As for Zenith in general, Barrie locals on Reddit spoke well of it, with comments like "Zenith is great" and "pretty good." Review aggregator Yably shows it held consistently strong ratings on Google.

The only real caution we came across was second-hand: one Reddit commenter said they'd heard a couple of Zenith's rooms were "crazy hard" and the experience overall was pretty intense.
