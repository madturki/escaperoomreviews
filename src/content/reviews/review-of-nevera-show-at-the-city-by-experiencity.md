We loved visiting The City in Madrid where the experience begins before you even enter. Once you are let inside you find yourself in a futuristic city where each escape room is a building you’ll find as you walk down the alley. What this means is that you may run into other players as they roam “the streets” trying to solve their own puzzles. It’s a really cool experience and as of almost 150 rooms, we’ve never seen anything like this.

Nevera Show is not your typical escape room but it does have the same sort of puzzle elements you loved combined with an incredible experience hosted by your game master. Our host Marina was fantastic in this room just as she was in the other. She orchestrated the experience for us ensuring we were having the most fun possible.

This room has a big variety of games and mini games. We played with three but two or four players would be better as there is communication involved. Our host told us that two players is ideal.

You will finish this room as it's meant to be a TV show but who will win? As you compete head to head one of you will win the big check while the other will, sorry, die.

There are some elements that could be presented better here but there's a good combo of audio, video, and lighting. Don't forget the secret phrase.
