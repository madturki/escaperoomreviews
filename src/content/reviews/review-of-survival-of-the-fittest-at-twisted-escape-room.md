_After being kidnapped by a serial killer who believes only the smartest should shape humanity's future, you wake up in an abandoned warehouse and find out that you have been injected with poison. You have one hour to locate the antidote and get out of there._

We have not yet completed a review of Survival of the Fittest at Twisted Escape Room in Mississauga. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A low-tech kidnapping room that reviewers describe as fine but unremarkable.

Reviewers on Morty mention some inventive physical puzzles, an interesting teamwork puzzle and very friendly staff. The We Try To Escape blog liked the variety and flow and singled out two genuinely memorable puzzles.

Morty and We Try To Escape both describe a basic set. The blog noted a gap between the walls and ceiling that let in noise from other rooms, no background music, and puzzles only loosely tied to the story. Several reviewers call it easy and linear, and one Morty reviewer found a puzzle misleading because it required moving something players are told not to move.
