## What others are saying

Witness is a 90-minute memory-dive detective game at INBLACK's North York location, and opinions on Morty are sharply split.

Positive Morty reviewers enjoyed the mechanical locks, magnets and hands-on puzzles, the gradually rising difficulty and the two-part structure, where clues from the story-driven first section feed into a second puzzle room. A Google reviewer who came for date night liked the mix of storytelling and puzzle solving.

Negative Morty reviewers describe a dirty, smoky-smelling venue, a dark room, a subtitled video carrying much of the story, music loud enough to drown out clues and hosts who were hard to understand. One said a heavy door frame started to topple at the end, and some reviewers accuse the company of posting inflated reviews.
