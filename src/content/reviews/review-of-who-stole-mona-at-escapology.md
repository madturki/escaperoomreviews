You manage to sneak away from the guards and are able to hide out until the gallery closes, but now you must use your teamwork to uncover its location and make it out before Stepanov’s guards discover you._

## What others are saying

A single-room art heist set in a Russian billionaire's private gallery, with a mostly positive but divided reception.

Reviewers on Morty love the art-gallery atmosphere and single out the laser-maze grand finale, and several found it a good, linear room for couples. A Tripadvisor reviewer loved the technical effects and the storyline.

Criticism centres on puzzle logic. Morty reviewers mention a couple of logical leaps, arbitrary ordering, odd wording and misleading elements, plus one puzzle that pulls from all over the room and a low-energy final puzzle after the big heist moment. The Tripadvisor reviewer doubted they would have finished without hints.
