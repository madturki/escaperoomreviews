## What others are saying

An old-school explorer's-study room, formerly called The Explorer's Study II.

A reviewer on Morty enjoyed the many old-school locks, the amount of puzzles packed in and a difficulty level suited to slightly rusty players. A Google reviewer called it a fantastic room, and a Yelp reviewer who played it as The Explorer's Study II found it well designed.

The Morty reviewer felt one puzzle involved too much maths.
