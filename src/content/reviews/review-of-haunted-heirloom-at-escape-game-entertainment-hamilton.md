_Spend the night in the haunted Hawthorne Estate, solving mysteries to claim your inheritance before dawn. The spirits of the manor are restless, and dark secrets lurk in every shadow. Can you uncover the truth behind the family curse and escape with your legacy—and your life?_

We have not yet completed a review of Haunted Heirloom at Escape Game Entertainment in Hamilton. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A haunted-manor inheritance mystery (previously run as Room 1408) that comes across as a modest but workable room.

Reviewers on Morty who went in hesitant were pleasantly surprised, calling it cute, clean and clever, with puzzles that made sense and were all in working order. Another Morty reviewer said it felt different from other escape experiences and that the puzzles were solid.

The same reviewers also found it nothing spectacular, a little shabby and under-decorated, and one missed the satisfaction of actually opening locks. A Google reviewer who played every room at the venue was disappointed to finish Haunted Heirloom by opening an empty safe with no heirloom inside, and felt the rooms overall were bare and lacked a clear ending.
