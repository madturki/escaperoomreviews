The custom built plants are elaborate and fairly incredible. The rest of the structures were also really well designed and built. I like the chaotic feel of some of it - the plants were taking over! The space is probably 25% larger than it needs to be. Having a more dense space-based botanical lab feels to make more sense to me and would make moving around the empty spaces less utilitarian.

The puzzles were a mix of searching, physical problem solving, a few traditional locks, some memorization, and some reaction times. There were a couple of unique puzzles as well which we enjoyed. Generally, the puzzles were what we'd call a medium difficulty. There was one puzzle with a redundant set of clues that we didn't really need. There were a few time consuming recordings that built out the story but weren't really helpful in solving and escaping. One of the "wow" pieces didn't work as intended which was disappointing.

This was our sixth room of the day and our brains were tired so we missed some things that should've been obvious. There were a few times where we stood looking at each other wondering "what next" because there isn't a clear path forward. It's a very open-world problem solving situation and you'll have to really investigate the space well. We estimated that we wasted probably 30 minutes just staring at each other and searching for items that were in plain sight. By the end of the game, with just two of us, we completed the room in just 1.5 minutes over the 75 minute time limit. We were also told about one of the items "yeah people miss that a lot." To me, that means that some things are probably unclear for others.

This room is large. You could fit twelve people in here if you wanted to. Four to six people would do well here since there's so much to explore. Daydream's experiences are quite costly at $46 per person and we think this room leans a bit too much into beautiful props and misses some of the things that traditionally make a great escape room. It felt a bit unfinished (especially for Daydream) and you weren't on a mission as much as in a sandbox.

It's not their best room and we nudge you towards checking out [Brimley Manor](/review-of-brimley-manor-at-daydream-adventures/) unless you're really into plants... Then this is for you!
