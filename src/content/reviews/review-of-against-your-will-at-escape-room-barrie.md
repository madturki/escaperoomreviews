We have not yet completed a review of Against Your Will at Escape Room Barrie in Barrie. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Against Your Will is still running at Escape Room Barrie's downtown location, and the venue's own escape-rate figures suggest it's one of its tougher games. Feedback is positive, though there isn't a lot of detailed commentary about this room specifically.

An experienced player on Morty loved the theming and the non-linear layout, which lets a team split up or switch to another puzzle when one has them stuck. A player on Reddit admitted their family didn't escape in time but said they had a very good time "being frustrated" and planned to come back for the other rooms. A TripAdvisor reviewer playing their first escape room with a group of five called it well thought out and left wanting to do another. More generally, Barrie locals on Reddit describe the rooms as designed by owners who love escape rooms.

We didn't find any real complaints about this room. The main things to know are that it's challenging, so many groups don't make it out, and the kidnapping theme is spooky enough that the venue says it isn't for kids under 8. Difficulty is in the eye of the beholder, though: the same Morty player found it moderate compared with the venue's other rooms.
