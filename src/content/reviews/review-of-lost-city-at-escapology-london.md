_Archibald Tucker was one of the greatest explorers of the 1930s. He uncovered a treasure so rare, no man alive had seen it! But a sudden gust of wind slammed the temple door shut - sealing Tucker and his journal inside and both were never seen again. Once you’re inside, angered spirits awaken and deem you unworthy of the riches held within…Now, as a test, they grant you 60 minutes to prove your worth._

## What others are saying

A 1930s jungle-temple treasure hunt that reviewers enjoy as an immersive, family-friendly room.

The We Try To Escape blog praised the realistic, well-maintained set and strong immersion, with logical, on-theme puzzles of moderate difficulty. A reviewer on Morty singled out an impressive set piece right at the start, and Google reviewers describe the puzzles as challenging and subtle but not impossible.

The blog notes that it is a linear room, so only one puzzle can be worked on at a time. The Morty reviewer found a few puzzles a slight logic stretch and hit a broken sensor near the end that had to be triggered manually, though staff added time to make up for it.
