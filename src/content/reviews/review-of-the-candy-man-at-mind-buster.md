_You have been invited to experience what goes on inside Godis Hyde’s spectacular Candy Shoppe of Wonders! However, you soon find out that the candy is not what it seems and there are many secrets going on behind the scenes. Can you make it out in time, or will you fall to his maniacal recipes?_

We have not yet completed a review of The Candy Man at Mind Buster in Barrie. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

The Candy Man appears to have been retired. Morty lists the room as permanently closed, and World of Escapes marks both The Candy Man and Mind Buster's other room, The Jewel of Excalibur, as closed, which suggests the venue itself has shut down.

We couldn't find many reviews of this room specifically, but what we did find was positive. Player ratings on Morty were mostly positive across gameplay, atmosphere and customer service, and a player there said they enjoyed it and that communication is key. It was described as a mechanical, non-scary room with no live actors. A TripAdvisor reviewer who booked The Candy Man called Mind Buster clean, friendly and "challenging yet fair and fun," and enjoyed it so much they went straight into The Jewel of Excalibur afterwards.

Feedback on Mind Buster in general was similarly warm, with TripAdvisor reviewers praising clever, challenging puzzles, helpful clues when needed and a friendly owner who let a group keep playing past the time limit when the room wasn't booked afterwards. We didn't come across any notable complaints about the room or the venue.
