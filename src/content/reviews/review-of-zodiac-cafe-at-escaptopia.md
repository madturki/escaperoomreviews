## What others are saying

A cosy, tech-driven café-management room that reviewers find delightful, especially for small groups and beginners.

Reviewers on Morty love the cute props and story, very clear signposting, and side puzzles that earn coins for hints, with a lot packed into the time. Google reviewers describe a beautifully decorated room with clear audio, and say it's great for kids and novices.

Morty reviewers note it is a small, linear space best for two or three, with an occasional delay before the next part activates. The sign outside shows a different business name, so it can be hard to spot.
