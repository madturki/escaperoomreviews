_The Barn is located on an actual farm built in the late 1870’s. It is NOT a warehouse unit that has been artificially made to look like the real thing like many other escape rooms. This single room is 2,000 square feet and has both natural and artificial light. Thick stone walls with large wooden posts and beams authenticate the cozy country theme._

We have not yet completed a review of The Barn at Let Me Out in Mono. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Reviewers on Morty and TripAdvisor describe The Barn as a distinctive, handmade escape room set inside a real old barn, run by a host who stays with players throughout the game.

Reviewers on Morty praised the unique set-up and its handmade feel compared with traditional fabricated rooms, calling the puzzles well thought out and challenging, with a good flow that let teams keep a steady pace. They also found the barn setting very atmospheric and liked the colour-coded difficulty levels. TripAdvisor reviewers echoed this, calling the puzzles very well thought out, noting that the space is large compared with other rooms, and singling out host Paul for staying in character. They also said the four difficulty levels make it worth replaying, and that it suits both beginners and experienced players, as well as larger groups split into teams.

Criticism is minimal. One Morty reviewer noted that there is not much technology, though they felt the room made it work without high-tech gadgets. They also said the game master stays in the barn but mostly keeps to himself unless needed. Another reviewer said the room is especially suited to bigger groups, although a pair still managed to escape.
