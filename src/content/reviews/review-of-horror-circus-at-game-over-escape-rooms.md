## What others are saying

A spooky cursed-circus room that many reviewers consider the better of this venue's horror rooms.

Reviewers on Morty praise the fun use of tech, jump scares, logical flow and puzzles that need players to coordinate. Google reviewers call it very interactive, well set up and properly scary.

Some Morty players found a few puzzles janky and needed many hints. One group had a marionette that did not work properly and a timer that kept running after they had finished.
