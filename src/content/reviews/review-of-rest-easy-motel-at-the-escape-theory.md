## What others are saying

A creepy roadside-motel room, the toughest at the venue, and a reviewer favourite.

Reviewers on Morty praise varied, satisfying puzzles, lots of searching and surprises, a mix of mechanical and tech elements, and an optional live actor that rattled one group. Google reviewers call it eerie, intense and full of clever puzzles with a chilling ending, and one experienced player ranked it among the best rooms they've done.

Morty reviewers mention a cramped space, very dark areas that need flashlights, a puzzle that had been set up upside down, tripping hazards, and some flow problems, including a near sequence break. Keeping track of all the keys and locks also takes time.
