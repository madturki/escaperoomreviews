## What others are saying

The licensed Scooby-Doo room at Escapology London is a colourful, family-friendly mystery that most players enjoy, though enthusiasts are less impressed.

The We Try To Escape blog liked the cartoon-style décor packed with Scooby-Doo details and the fun, story-driven puzzles, which use automatic mechanisms rather than padlocks, and recommends it for families and small groups. Google reviewers praise the consistent, professional theming, functional props and interactive puzzles.

A reviewer on Morty found it cute and nostalgic but said the puzzles were straightforward, the set was nothing special and there was only one unique puzzle, and their group finished very quickly. The blog felt the first two rooms lacked a clear purpose beyond decoration and found one object placement illogical.
