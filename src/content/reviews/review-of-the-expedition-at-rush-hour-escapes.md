## What others are saying

The Expedition is a jungle-temple hunt for a crystal skull and is the most-reviewed room at Rush Hour Escapes. Reception is consistently positive, especially from families.

Tripadvisor reviewers praise the lighting and decor, the challenging puzzles that reward teamwork and attention to detail, and cryptic clues that still don't frustrate younger players. Several families played with children as young as seven. Staff get frequent mentions for being welcoming, giving the right amount of guidance, and letting a group of three play a room meant for four to eight. Google reviewers likewise call it challenging but fun, with some sweet surprises.

Complaints are rare. One Tripadvisor reviewer points out that the entry stairs could be a barrier for anyone with accessibility needs.
