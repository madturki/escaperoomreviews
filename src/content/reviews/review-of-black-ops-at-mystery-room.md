_Our Special Agents obtained evidence against the international arms dealer The Hawk, your squad has been sent to make the capture. You attack the bunker identified as The Hawk’s HQ but it is a trap. The Hawk is nowhere to be found and the location is booby-trapped and alarmed. You must disarm or work around the traps and alarms while searching for anything that can help find where The Hawk is now._

We have not yet completed a review of Black Ops at Mystery Room in Buffalo. Please see our [review of "Hollywood Premiere" at Mystery Room Buffalo](/review-of-hollywood-premiere-at-escape-the-mystery-room/) for an idea of their experiences.

## What others are saying

Escape the Mystery Room at the Walden Galleria (later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) appears to have closed for good. TripAdvisor lists the location as permanently closed and Morty describes the venue as no longer operating, so Black Ops can no longer be played here.

We couldn't find many reviews of Black Ops specifically. A player review on the room's EscapeRoom.com listing called the clues clever and said it made for a fun family afternoon, though the room wasn't quite what they expected and they would have preferred not to share it with another group.

Feedback on the venue in general was mixed. TripAdvisor reviewers often praised the friendly, helpful staff and liked it as a puzzle outing with no horror elements. Critics described small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers full of combination locks, unclear instructions, hard-to-read clues and locks that wouldn't open with the right code. On Reddit, an experienced Buffalo player advised skipping it, calling it too expensive for "a little office" and noting that a box they opened by guessing held the clue meant to open it.
