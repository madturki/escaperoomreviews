## What others are saying

An archaeology-themed room, said to be the hardest at this location, with sharply split reviews.

One reviewer on Morty found the puzzles interesting, thematic and tougher than the venue's other rooms, and recommends it along with Cursed Dungeon. Google reviewers praise unique, challenging puzzles and the atmosphere, and a group of teens at a birthday had a great time.

Morty reviewers say the final puzzle kills the momentum because only two people can work on it while everyone else watches. Others called the puzzles illogical and the game masters dismissive.
