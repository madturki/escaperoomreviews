_Archibald Tucker was one of the greatest explorers of the 1930s. He uncovered a treasure so rare, no man alive had seen it! But a sudden gust of wind slammed the temple door shut - sealing Tucker and his journal inside and both were never seen again. Once you’re inside, angered spirits awaken and deem you unworthy of the riches held within…Now, as a test, they grant you 60 minutes to prove your worth._

## What others are saying

An Indiana Jones-style temple adventure that reviewers describe as an immersive, approachable room for families and mixed-experience groups.

Reviewers on Morty praise the well-thought-out décor, props that match the theme and puzzles that are challenging yet intuitive, with a nice mix of tech and hands-on tasks. A Tripadvisor reviewer who played with three teens called it full of cool challenges and twists, with just the right level of difficulty.

On the downside, a Morty reviewer who played during the soft launch hit miscues and puzzle issues that hurt the flow and felt the overhead lights were too bright. The Tripadvisor reviewer found it a little pricey for an hour, with no discount for kids.
