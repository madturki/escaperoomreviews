## What others are saying

Last Resort is a horror room set in a remote motel, and reviewers on Morty and Google reviewers rate it among the venue's best.

Morty reviewers praise the fun, challenging puzzles, the tech and the effects, and one called it the scariest room they had played, in creative ways. Google reviewers who played it alongside The Ark and Noriko named it a favourite, and some enjoyed it so much they booked a second room straight away.

The main criticism on Morty is flow: players had to go back and forth between spaces, and it wasn't always clear what to do next. Some also felt the final choice was rushed, and one reviewer found the motel theme felt like an afterthought.
