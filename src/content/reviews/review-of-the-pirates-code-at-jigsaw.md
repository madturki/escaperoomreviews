This was one of the best rooms I’ve ever been in.  Pirate-themed rooms have really stepped up their game over the years.  This room is full of modern technical integrations that enhance the room without breaking the immersion.

The puzzles are a great blend of styles and difficulties from physical, to riddles, to spatial and memory-based. The technology is well-hidden or integrated and doesn't affect immersion. It is a very new room and everything is in excellent condition still.

The staff and location are fantastic and you’ll want to get there early and enjoy a beer with your friends.  This room would be great with up to six people but can work with as few as two.

You’ll definitely want to book right away and make this a stop while in the Byward Market. Really – don’t miss this one! This room made our list of the [Best Immersive Escape Rooms](/most-immersive-escape-rooms-in-ontario/).
