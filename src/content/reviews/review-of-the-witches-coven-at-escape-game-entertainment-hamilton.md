## What others are saying

A witchy initiation room with an easy, linear flow that has left most reviewers disappointed.

One Google reviewer called it fun and intricate, not too hard but not easy either, and a good pick for beginners because the puzzles are linear, with a friendly, welcoming manager. A Morty reviewer also said the host was nice and did his best.

Most feedback is negative, though. Reviewers on Morty describe a messy, DIY-looking room that did not match the photos, with disjointed, simple puzzles and a final room that failed to open. Google reviewers report fragile props, a wand held together with electrical tape that broke, missing pieces of recipe plaques, glitchy puzzles, and a game that timed out before one group could solve the last puzzle.
