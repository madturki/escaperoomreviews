## What others are saying

Elevator Pitch appears to be retired. It was a limited-run room based on Linwood Barclay's thriller of the same name, built inside a custom elevator car with voiceovers from Barclay himself, and was announced to run for one year starting in October 2019. It no longer appears in Escape Manor Toronto's current lineup.

We couldn't find any player reviews of this room specifically. Coverage at the time focused on the novelty of the tie-in: a short, story-driven game for a small group, with puzzles drawn from the book.

As for Escape Manor's Toronto location in general, reviews of its other rooms praise the production value, the friendly staff and the lounge, where you can grab a drink before your game or even order one into the room. Some experienced players note that many of the puzzles will feel familiar if you've done a lot of escape rooms.
