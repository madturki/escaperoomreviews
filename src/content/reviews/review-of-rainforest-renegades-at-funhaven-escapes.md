We have not yet completed a review of Rainforest Renegades at Funhaven Escapes in Ottawa. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Rainforest Renegades is a 60-minute, multi-room jungle adventure inside the Funhaven family entertainment centre. It has been covered by a Morty reviewer and by two blogs, Escape Reviewer and Escape Around the World, though the blog reviews date from several years ago.

Escape Reviewer praised the surprising way players enter the game, the large multi-storey layout, teamwork-heavy puzzles and an unusually satisfying thematic ending. Escape Around the World found the puzzles diverse and well suited to families, with cerebral puzzles for adults and hands-on ones for kids, plus some remarkable props and a well-staged exit. A reviewer on Morty found it an entertaining addition to an arcade visit.

The Morty reviewer felt the room was fine but not worth a dedicated trip. Escape Around the World flagged one ambiguous listening puzzle, could hear children in the park during a busy daytime slot and disliked the host entering the room to give hints. They recommend at least three players.
