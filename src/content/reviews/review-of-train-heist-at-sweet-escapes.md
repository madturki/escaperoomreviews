## What others are saying

Train Heist at Sweet Escapes is a gem-theft room set in the bar car of a luxury train. The little feedback available so far is positive.

A reviewer on Morty counted it among their favourite rooms at the venue, praising the mix of puzzle types and difficulty levels, a few clever solves, and enough to do to keep a whole group busy. They also found the staff delightful. A Google reviewer called it challenging and a lot of fun, and enjoyed picking out treats from the candy shop afterwards.

The Morty reviewer cautioned that it's one large room and fairly low-tech, so players expecting elaborate sets or effects should adjust their expectations. With only a handful of reviews so far, there isn't yet a broad consensus on the room.
