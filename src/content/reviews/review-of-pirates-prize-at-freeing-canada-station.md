_To gain entry onto the legendary ghost ship, you track down the grizzled old captain and hurl the most creative insults at his mother. Enraged, he throws you into the quartermaster's cramped quarters: "You'll walk the plank at sunrise." Somewhere within this multi-room pirate ship lies a fabled artifact of immense power. Find it and escape before dawn._

We have not yet completed a review of Pirate's Prize at Freeing Canada Station in Richmond Hill. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A multi-room pirate-ship adventure and the best-received game at this venue.

Reviewers on Morty praise varied puzzles, good flow and tech, solid theming and very friendly staff, and call it a good beginner-to-intermediate room. Google reviewers describe immersive elements, unique contraptions that need real teamwork, a clean venue and something for every teen in the group, with more production value than Unmask.

Morty reviewers mention one unclear puzzle, a tricky maths puzzle, and wishing it were longer.
