_Two years ago, a brilliant university biology student ran secret experiments combining human and animal genes in an underground laboratory, and silenced a fellow student who discovered his work. A few days ago, you and your fellow biology students received an anonymous text directing you to a secret underground operation room. You have 60 minutes to unveil the truth._

We have not yet completed a review of Unmask at Freeing Canada Station in Richmond Hill. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A dark, horror-tinged lab room with mixed feedback.

A reviewer on Morty enjoyed many of the puzzles, and a Google reviewer called it challenging and well thought out.

The Morty reviewer lost about ten minutes to a mechanical issue and found one hard-to-read symbol in the low light unfair, noting wear and tear across the venue. The Google reviewer preferred Pirate's Prize for its production value.
