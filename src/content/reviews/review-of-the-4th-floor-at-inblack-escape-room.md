## What others are saying

An intense, 90-minute live-actor horror room that many players call the scariest they've done.

Reviewers on Morty praise the immersive set, well-executed scares, passionate actors and an unexpectedly emotional story. Players on Reddit called it awesome, and Google reviewers say the scares felt real and kept them on edge.

Several Morty reviewers say there are few real puzzles, so it can feel more like a haunted house than an escape room. One noted that public booking can put you with strangers, and that their group had to share a single light in the dark sections.
