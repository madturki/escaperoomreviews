In this immersive experience, the curtain rises on a wartime theatre where nothing is as it seems. Behind the velvet curtains, sinister forces conspire, and it’s up to you and your team to uncover their hidden agenda. Can you navigate the shadowy world of backstage intrigue, decode secret messages, and alter the course of history?

We haven't yet had a chance to complete A Spy is Born at Camp Picton in Picton but hope to complete it soon.

## What others are saying

Escape Camp Picton still lists this room as "A Spy is Born". We couldn't find any detailed reviews of it specifically. Below is what people say about Escape Camp Picton in general.

Visitors are very positive about the company overall. Google reviewers often mention the unique setting in a real WWII air force barrack at Base 31, puzzles that are challenging but fair, friendly hosts (one even in costume), and games that work well for families and beginners. Enthusiasts on the Morty app praised the venue's other rooms for their historical immersion and clever, low-tech "aha" moments. A German enthusiast blog, Escape Roomers, enjoyed the gamemastering and found their game a good challenge.

Common complaints are fairly minor. One Morty player hoped for more immersion given the historic location, found the space small, and felt the game master coming into the room to give hints broke the flow. Another noted a few red herrings, and the Escape Roomers blog mentioned a fair number of padlocks (though they felt it suited the theme). A Google reviewer also said the rooms can get quite warm on hot summer days.
