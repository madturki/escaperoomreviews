## What others are saying

A charming, family-friendly fantasy room that reviewers recommend for beginners and families as well as more experienced players.

Reviewers on Morty call it delightful from start to finish, with a mix of puzzle difficulties and types, classic locks alongside more high-tech elements, and a points system with optional, non-linear bonus puzzles so there is always something to work on. Google reviewers praise clever puzzles, great hosts and a beautifully crafted set, and say it is more doable than the venue's Professor's Will. Staff and owners are repeatedly described as friendly and passionate.

The main reservation is that it is fairly standard fare: one Morty reviewer described the theming as having a children's play room vibe and the gameplay as typical code-and-box solving, though not in a bad way. Some first-time groups on Google also say they only made it part of the way through.
