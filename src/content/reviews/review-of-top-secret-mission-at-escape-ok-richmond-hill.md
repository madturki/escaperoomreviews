## What others are saying

A spy-and-lasers room that reviewers mostly describe as decent but unexceptional.

Reviewers on Morty say the puzzles made sense and everything worked as expected. Google reviewers enjoyed the laser section and the high-tech elements, found it roomy enough for a large group, and say it gets better as it goes.

Morty players found it fairly standard and the space a bit dull. Google reviewers mention a dark opening section with weak or faulty flashlights and a time-sensitive puzzle that felt unfair.
