**Word on the street is that this room may be going away soon! I would just go ahead and book it now!**

This room was the first we'd done that really had us feeling edgy.  The Immersion begins from the very start as the enthusiastic host set the scene.  This person-to-person storytelling is much more immersive than video introductions offered up at most venues and we love it. The involvement from the staff and personalized puzzles (you'll see what we mean) contribute to the high overall score.  

Once inside the setting and puzzles were unique and eerie making us let out little yelps from time to time.  Though there are a few puzzles that are a slight departure from the theme (more paranormal than natural) they're very well-thought-out overall. Noise from the nearby axe-throwing does occasionally break the immersion (you should check it out while you're there!)

We gave this a medium difficulty level and it's suitable for groups from 3 to 6 people.

Overall, this was one of our favourite rooms and a definite one for you to check out!
