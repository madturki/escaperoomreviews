Time is of the essence since you only have until midnight before he claims his next victim. Can you stop the Crossroads killer?_

We have not yet completed a review of Crossroads at Escape Games Canada in Toronto. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Crossroads is a dark detective thriller set in an abandoned subway station. Reviewers on Morty, Room Escape Artist and Google reviewers generally consider it a solid, atmospheric game.

Morty reviewers repeatedly call the set gorgeous and immersive, and several enjoyed the choice that has real consequences for the puzzles. Room Escape Artist also singled out that choice mechanic, which affects both the story and the difficulty, and Google reviewers who played it alongside other rooms here enjoyed it just as much.

On the downside, Morty reviewers note the puzzles are easier and more linear than in the venue's other rooms, and one felt the final puzzle went from challenging to unfun. Room Escape Artist found the indexing-heavy puzzles not especially mind-bending, said the game is more about the experience, and dealt with a jammed combination lock early in their game.
