## What others are saying

A single-room, narrative-heavy thriller that divides players.

Reviewers on Morty call the story outstanding and an emotional roller coaster, and praise the tech, set design and special effects. Google reviewers say the storyline is among the best they've played and that there are enough puzzles to fill the time.

Morty reviewers felt some parts lacked direction, some puzzles were repetitive, and much of it is following the narrator. One Google reviewer was disappointed: staying in one room felt boring, the decor seemed low-budget and the story felt juvenile.
