## What others are saying

A dungeon prison-break room that reviewers generally like for its atmosphere and fresh puzzles.

Reviewers on Morty enjoyed the vibe, some puzzles they hadn't seen before, and the teamwork it demands, calling it a good pick for two couples. A Google reviewer said the build felt like a real dungeon and liked the tech and challenging puzzles.

Morty players report inputs that didn't register, a broken prop that needed the game master, and a puzzle that is physically uncomfortable for the tallest player. One reviewer believes it has been revamped from 90 to 60 minutes.
