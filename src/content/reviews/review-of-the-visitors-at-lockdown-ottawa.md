We have not yet completed a review of The Visitors at Lockdown Ottawa in Ottawa. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

The Visitors is a short, alien-themed first-generation room at Lockdown that starts in total darkness. Reviewers on Morty, Tripadvisor and the We Try To Escape blog generally see it as a good fit for beginners.

A Tripadvisor reviewer enjoyed the fun props, décor and a mechanical element, and found it suitable for families with children. We Try To Escape liked starting in the dark, appreciated a few unique puzzles and noted that the low difficulty suits newer players. A reviewer on Morty describes it as short and sweet.

We Try To Escape found the décor basic and the first room a little empty. They also say there isn't enough content, the game is linear and there is no locker for belongings. The Tripadvisor reviewer questioned how hygienic the shared props were.
