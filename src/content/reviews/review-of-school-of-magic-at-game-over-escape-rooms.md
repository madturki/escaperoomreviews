## What others are saying

A wand-driven wizarding-school room that reviewers find pleasant and family-friendly but on the easy side.

Reviewers on Morty like being taught to use a wand by the game master before the game and then using it throughout, and say the tech worked well and the solutions felt fair. Google reviewers describe it as very enjoyable with good tech, and families mention having a great time.

Morty players note that it is linear and easy, and that the first few spaces feel sparse, with little beyond the props you need. One group had lasers that did not line up and found the wand awkward to use.
