We have not yet completed a review of Saw at Exitus Escape Rooms in Guelph. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Saw is a horror-themed race to find an antidote and is Exitus's hardest room. Reviewers find it tough and mostly enjoyable.

Reviewers on Morty call the puzzles challenging but fair, praise the friendly staff, and like the mix of mechanical and tech puzzles. One notes that the many locks never feel like simply opening the next box. A Google reviewer found it just the right amount of challenge. A Tripadvisor reviewer lost but still had an amazing time.

On Morty, players warn there is a lot of maths and plenty of "time wasters", and say it's often unclear which lock a solution belongs to. They also found the second room dim and the theme not very scary. One group that was booked on hard mode without knowing it failed the room, and reported lights that weren't working and a missing item.
