## What others are saying

A scary, adults-only serial-killer house at Hidden Trail's Mind Over Matter location, with a mostly positive but uneven reception.

Reviewers on Morty and Tripadvisor found it challenging but not impossible, with good flow and just the right amount of creepiness. Google reviewers praise the well-made puzzles, the mix of scary and fun, and game masters who give just enough help for things to click.

One Morty reviewer had a very poor visit, reporting exposed wiring, a game that had been reset incorrectly, walkie-talkies that did not work and little flow. A Tripadvisor reviewer also found the location confusing to find because it operates under the Mind Over Matter name.
