## What others are saying

A light-hearted room built around the TV show Friends that reviewers consistently find fun, especially if they're fans.

Reviewers on Morty love the Easter eggs and inside jokes worked into the puzzles, the clear objective and the smooth progression. The We Try To Escape blog enjoyed the many theme-linked puzzles and the non-linear layout, calling the puzzles the highlight.

The puzzles are on the easy side, and both Morty reviewers and the blog say the set isn't very polished. The blog noted a prop held together with duct tape and being able to hear neighbouring rooms. Some familiarity with the show helps.
