_Gotham City, a metropolis plagued by crime and injustice, is in dire need of heroes. Batman has initiated a new Gotham City Guardian training program, and you have been selected to stop a training device planted in the heart of Gotham City. The device is fitted with a countdown clock that will release the gas in exactly 60 minutes, so you'll have to work quickly._

## What others are saying

A licensed Batman training-simulation room that reviewers describe as one of the harder games at Escapology Waterloo, with impressive sets.

Reviewers on Morty rave about the theming, from the Batcave onward, the unique puzzles and the clever use of familiar villains, and note that no prior Batman knowledge is needed. Several called it immersive and more challenging than they expected.

That difficulty cuts both ways: a player on Reddit found it too hard for children aged eight and ten. Morty reviewers also mention slightly off voice acting for Batman, uncertainty about which props are meant to be opened, and one group whose game was derailed by a malfunction that forced them to use hints.
