> Welcome to the Queen Street Manor; the home of the Italian restaurateur ‘Mama’ Rosa Napoli, the genius behind the famous franchise Napoli. Last night, after a wonderful celebration of Mama Napoli’s retirement for her many years of dedication to her family and business, she was found murdered somewhere on the estate. You must help us beat the local authorities and find the killer and the murder weapon before they arrive in an hour!

We have not yet completed a review of Whodunnit "The Lounge" at Breakout in Niagara. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Whodunnit: The Lounge appears to be closed. World of Escapes lists it as closed at BreakOUT Escapes' former Queen Street address, and it isn't among the venue's current Niagara Falls rooms. We couldn't find any reviews of this room specifically.

The closest feedback we found is for Whodunnit "Dining", a sister room at the same Niagara Falls venue with the same Mama Napoli murder mystery. Morty also lists that room as permanently closed. One enthusiast described it as small, simple and fun, if nothing revolutionary. Another strongly disliked it, calling it a lock-heavy room with poorly designed, unfair puzzles and lots of red herrings.

If you'd like to try the storyline, WhereToEscape lists a Whodunnit "Dining Room" at BreakOUT's Cambridge (Galt) location.
