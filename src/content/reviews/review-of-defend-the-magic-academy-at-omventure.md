> For centuries, Ilverward’s School for Gifted Young Mages has stood as a grand academy built upon a place of power, attracting new Mages from near and far to learn magic. However, in recent times, evil creatures and dark forces have begun to threaten the academy. The Professors and the Headmaster find themselves on the front lines, defending the Magic Academy even as a new school year begins.
> 
> As new mages, you will embark on an adventure, mastering lessons along the way to acquire the abilities necessary to aid your academy in reclaiming a powerful relic known as the Stone of Wisdom. Once discovered and properly placed, the Stone of Wisdom will restore the protective force field around the school, securing the future of the academy once and for all.
