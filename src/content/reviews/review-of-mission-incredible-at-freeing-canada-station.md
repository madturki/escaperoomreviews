The government's secret services have recruited you for a mission that does not exist: infiltrate the museum, bypass its defences, and steal the diamond first._

## What others are saying

A laser-heist museum room built around physical, agility-style puzzles.

Reviewers on Morty enjoy the lasers and the physical puzzles and call it a well-done standard room. An older review on the Escape Games Review blog praised the sturdiest laser design they had seen and cleverly hidden elements.

Morty reviewers say the final tactile puzzle has to be reset by staff every time you get it wrong. One had a very negative visit, including a staff member physically guiding them without asking and tidying the room mid-game. The blog found it cramped at maximum group size.
