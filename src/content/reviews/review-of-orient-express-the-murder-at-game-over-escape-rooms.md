## What others are saying

A train-carriage murder mystery that impresses with its set but splits opinion on its puzzles.

Reviewers on Morty love the train set, especially the dining car, and mention creative puzzles, mostly mechanisms, plus some deduction as the story unfolds. Google reviewers call it gorgeous and immersive, and one was told it is among the venue's hardest rooms.

Morty reviewers say the small, linear compartments mean only two or three people can work at once, so big groups get crowded. Several found certain puzzles confusing or close to impossible, especially near the end, and suggest not being shy about hints.
