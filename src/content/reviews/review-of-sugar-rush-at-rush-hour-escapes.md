## What others are saying

Sugar Rush is a candy-shop room built around a stolen Golden Ticket, and reviewers find it a fun, well-built game.

A reviewer on Morty praises the room engineering, the mix of manual locks and tech puzzles, and puzzles that respect the theme and always make sense, and says it's ideal for about four players. On Tripadvisor, a group of five adults liked the design and puzzles and escaped with a few minutes to spare, and a family spanning ages 13 to 71 had a great time.

The Morty reviewer notes the room doesn't have a big-chain budget, though they felt the quality was still there. The rooms are up a flight of stairs, and one Tripadvisor group had trouble finding parking.
