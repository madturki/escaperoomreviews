## What others are saying

A horror-hotel room that has quickly become the standout at Game Over's Burlington location.

Google reviewers rave about the set design and attention to detail, a layout and storyline that made them work hard, and puzzles that fit the theme, with obvious scares that add to the fun without going over the top. Reviewers on Morty were surprised by how big the space is and enjoyed the jump scares and cool design elements from start to finish.

On the downside, a Morty reviewer found a couple of the puzzles tedious and doubted it would stump enthusiasts, while another said the on-screen hints were not in sync with where the group was in the game. Google reviewers note it is a bit on the pricey side, though one felt the quality made it worthwhile.
