## What others are saying

An Arabian Nights–style room (previously called One Thousand & One Nights) that is one of the better-received games at this location.

Reviewers on Morty praise the set, the 'aha' puzzles, the video elements and a couple of physical skill games, and several say it is the room they would recommend here. Google reviewers highlight the decor, unique puzzles and an interactive screen.

A few Morty groups had the game reset in the middle (staff gave the lost time back), one found a puzzle that could soft-lock, and another found a motion-sensing game did not work well for lighter players. It is described as linear and difficult for beginners, and a Google reviewer found some contraptions tiring and unintuitive for a small group.
