We've now been coming to Escape Manor Hamilton off and on since we hosted our wedding reception there four years ago. One thing that always stands out, no matter which location we go to, is how incredible the staff are. They consistently go above and beyond and put in the maximum effort (and seem to have fun while doing it).

This room is one a few that gives me the creeps. Even though you know they're just an actor, hosting a game, at a restaurant/bar, the builds and characters are so good that you lose yourself in the moment. There are some scarier, more intense moments, so I don't recommend it for younger groups. As a duo, we completed this room in time and found there was the right amount of stuff for us but you could easily do this room with four people. Any more and it may get too crowded.

The puzzles were very well thought out and fit the story line and theme perfectly. They were the perfect fit for a creepy room about a disappearing child. The room is really well built with great ambient lighting and some ambient audio. The puzzles are a good mix of physical locks and supernatural effects.

We really loved this room at the Hamilton location and are confident it would be great anywhere because, well, that's how Escape Manor rolls.
