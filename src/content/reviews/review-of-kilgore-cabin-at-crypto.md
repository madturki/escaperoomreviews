## What others are saying

Kilgore Cabin is a horror-themed room and one of Crypto's oldest games. We couldn't find any blog reviews of it, so this summary is based on player reviews on Morty. The general feeling is positive, though enthusiasts often see it as the least memorable room in Crypto's lineup.

Players like that the tech works smoothly, that it's all tech with no padlocks, and that the lighting and sound make it clear what to focus on. Several mentioned enjoying a few good jump scares, the creepy "Mother" theme and the flow from puzzle to puzzle, and one singled out the final space as a great set piece. Crypto's hosts also get praise for being friendly.

On the downside, many players found it easy, especially with a larger group, and some recommend going with a smaller team. A few felt the spaces were big but sparse and less detailed than Crypto's other rooms, and that the story was generic. Some hoped it would be scarier, and some players mentioned puzzles that came down to trial and error.
