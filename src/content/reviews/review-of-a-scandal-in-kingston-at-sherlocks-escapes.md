We completed this room back in 2023 and held off on posting the review. This room, like their others, was unique with some really cool elements. I was blown away when we walked in and with some of the surprise elements.

However, it was fraught with technical issues and errors in the puzzles themselves. It was one of few rooms we almost walked out of. **However, you may have a different experience than we did.**

It is encouraging to see that this year Sherlock's Escapes rebuilt several of their rooms and we hope that our feedback was incorporated into some of their designs.

We hope to return to reevaluate their new experiences.

**Feel free to experience the room yourself and let us know how it goes!**
