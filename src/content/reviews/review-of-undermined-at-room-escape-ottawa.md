## What others are saying

Undermined sends teams into an old mine to find out what happened to a missing geologist. It is a frequent favourite among reviewers on Morty and the We Try To Escape blog.

Reviewers on Morty highlight the unique use of space, impressive automated moments, surprising reveals and a strong variety of puzzles, with enough to keep a group of four busy. We Try To Escape calls the immersion extraordinary, citing realistic décor, a section of the game that is in motion and challenging non-linear puzzles. They highly recommend it as a family-friendly pick.

We Try To Escape didn't like how the story ended and notes that belongings stay in the room with you. On Morty, a couple of experienced pairs found it fun rather than truly difficult, and one reviewer felt their host was unfriendly.
