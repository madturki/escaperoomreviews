_You are incorrectly diagnosed with a bouquet of neurological impairments and deemed “a serious threat to society and self”. Luckily, you’re in the sweet, small town of Cherry Meadow. You just need someone to hear you out and things will turn out fine… Right? Work together with your fellow inpatients and escape – if you can._

We have not yet completed a review of Cherry Meadow Asylum at Hidden Trail Experiences in Windsor. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A creepy psychiatric-asylum room that divides players over its content.

Reviewers on Morty liked that it is creepy without being outright scary, and say it calls for good teamwork while still being escapable with a smaller group.

One Morty reviewer wished the room supplied a whiteboard or pen and paper for working out clues. More seriously, a Tripadvisor reviewer was upset by satanic imagery, including an altar and a pentagram, that they felt the room description gave no warning about, and their group left before time was up.
