We have not yet completed a review of Trudeau Towers at Lockdown Ottawa in Ottawa. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

Trudeau Towers is Lockdown's bomb-in-the-elevator room, billed as its most challenging. The few written reviews are positive.

Google reviewers call it fun and very well done, and one says a group of teens who had never played before loved it and nearly finished without a single hint. They also praise the helpful staff and clear rules briefing. A Tripadvisor reviewer who had played about 10 rooms in Ottawa and Toronto loved it, calling it a real challenge that they escaped with three minutes to spare.

The space is tight: one Google reviewer recommends it for groups of three at most.
