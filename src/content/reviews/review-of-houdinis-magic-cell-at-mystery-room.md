## What others are saying

Houdini's Magic Cell has been retired. Morty lists the room as permanently closed, and the venue itself (Escape the Mystery Room at the Walden Galleria, later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) is reported closed on both Morty and TripAdvisor.

We couldn't find any written reviews of this room specifically. Player ratings collected on Morty describe the overall reception as mixed: players were mostly positive about the customer service but negative about both the gameplay and the atmosphere. The room was billed as an expert-level challenge built around card tricks, handcuffs and padlocks.

Feedback on the venue in general followed a similar pattern. TripAdvisor reviewers praised the friendly staff and liked the no-horror format, while critics described small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers of combination locks, unclear instructions, hard-to-read clues and locks that didn't always work. An experienced player on Reddit advised skipping the venue, calling it overpriced for what it offered.
