A commander explains that they've lost contact with the crew at a secret military facility in your area, and an artificial intelligence within the facility is attempting to launch a nuclear missile. If you can't disable the AI and stop the missile launch, the air force will be ordered to destroy the facility._

## What others are saying

Bunker: AI's Martyrdom is a hacking-themed adventure set in a secret military bunker, and it gets an overwhelmingly positive reception on Morty. Room Escape Artist has also reviewed it.

Reviewers on Morty praise the realistic hacking, the convincing bunker set and a story some found genuinely moving. Room Escape Artist liked the in-game command centre, saying that once the team mastered the tech it truly felt like hacking in, and enjoyed the unusual, compelling story and the thrill of unlocking new spaces.

The most common complaint is the ending. Morty reviewers call the final task brutally hard, and Room Escape Artist felt two late-game puzzles, a physics problem and a repetitive data-entry task, overstayed their welcome. Room Escape Artist also flagged a lack of safety padding: large props left the team banged up, and one teammate hit his head on a low doorway.
