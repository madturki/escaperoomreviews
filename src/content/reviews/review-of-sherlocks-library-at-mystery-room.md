_Holmes is investigating a series of jewelry thefts that have targeted the English gentry.  He is preparing to play his violin, which he insists helps him think clearly_ while sending John Watson to investigate the offices of an accountant favored amongst the aristocracy. Holmes, fearful of discovery, gives _Watson **strict** instructions to only note what he finds and where and not to remove items from any of the files. Watson is just pleased to be away from 221b while Holmes plays!_
