_Every year, the Memory Retrieval Agency recruits new specialists. You're Kate, and you've arrived at the agency to take the entrance exam. The test involves helping three people fulfill their wishes. If you fail, you'll be sent back to your home planet._

We have not yet completed a review of Memory Retrieval Agency at Escaptopia in Richmond Hill. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A narrative, emotional room that leaves a strong impression either way.

Reviewers on Morty praise the adorable theming, fair puzzles that tie into the story, a twist, and a lot packed into a small set. One said it was the first time they had cried in an escape room. Google reviewers note it isn't meant to be scary.

Morty reviewers say the space is small and linear, best for a duo, and experienced players may find the puzzles easy. One Google reviewer was upset by animal-cruelty imagery shown in the ending video without warning, and another said the ending video is unsettling.
