The amulet has now gone missing, and Her Majesty has tasked you, her royal scribes, with searching Vesputin’s study to find the treasured item. She believes she can distract Vesputin for a single turn of the hourglass while you seek to recover her amulet and expose his treason!_

## What others are saying

A fantasy room in which players search a royal sorcerer's study for a stolen amulet; reviewers like the concept but are split on its condition.

Reviewers on Morty mention good props, fun technology and a nice set design, and some found it an enjoyable room with interesting puzzles. A Google reviewer had a really great time on a date night.

Maintenance is the recurring problem. Several Morty reviewers report props that were worn, malfunctioning, missing or not reset, with one group calling the whole game void, and others found some answers unclear and disliked losing time for hints. The Google reviewer reached the very last puzzle but could not get it to work because it was so finicky.
