## What others are saying

A tech-driven Atlantis expedition that players generally describe as a solid, good-looking room, with the usual caveats about value at this venue.

Reviewers on Morty enjoy the communication puzzles and the touches that make it feel like Atlantis, and several praise the mix of tech and mechanical puzzles and the effects. Google reviewers mention a beautifully decorated, immersive set and some unusually unique puzzles.

Several Morty reviewers say the puzzles will feel familiar to experienced players and that the room works best with four or fewer. One disliked that there are no walkie-talkies, so the game master comes into the room and solves the puzzle for you, and more than one felt the price is high for what you get.
