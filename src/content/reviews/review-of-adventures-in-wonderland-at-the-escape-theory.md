## What others are saying

A beginner-friendly Alice in Wonderland room that works well for kids' parties.

Reviewers on Morty like the cute props, puzzles that fit the theme and one especially fun feature, and adults enjoyed it as a fairy-tale adventure. Google reviewers describe a successful birthday party for a group of eight-year-olds.

Morty reviewers mention props that need repair, tripping hazards and some puzzles that didn't make sense. A Google reviewer's group didn't finish even though it's the easy room, and found the venue a little hard to find.
