## What others are saying

Mansion Murder at Escapology's Waterloo location is a classic whodunit set in a grand family estate, billed as one of the venue's toughest games. Reviewers mostly come away impressed, provided they go in ready for a real challenge.

Reviewers on Morty praise the theming, a well-dressed mansion spread across roughly three small rooms with mildly spooky touches, and say the puzzles are fewer in number but longer, logical and satisfying to crack; one calls it the best room at the location. A Groupon reviewer who played with a 12- and 10-year-old said everyone in the family was able to contribute, and noted the facility feels new and well kept.

The difficulty is the main caveat. Morty reviewers describe very wordy puzzles that involve cross-referencing several sources of information at once, which demands a lot of communication and can get frustrating if not everyone is fully engaged. Several groups leaned heavily on hints, and the Groupon reviewer's family escaped in the nick of time after multiple clues, while also calling the experience a bit pricey.
