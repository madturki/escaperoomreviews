## What others are saying

A lighter, story-driven horror room set in an orphanage.

Reviewers on Morty praise a tightly woven story and puzzles, effective lighting that guides your attention, tech that adds to the creepy mood, and a story staff share after you escape. Players on Reddit mention a few genuinely scary moments and suggest it as a fun date.

Morty reviewers say the space is small, best for two, with worn props that can feel like malfunctions. Some found it unremarkable, with only two rooms and mostly basic locks.
