## What others are saying

A small, dark horror room for two to four players in which you explore using camcorders.

Reviewers on Morty like how the night-vision camcorders add to the mood, find the puzzles fair, and praise its creepiness, especially for two players. Google reviewers single out Doll Island as a creepy favourite.

Morty reviewers note it is a very small space and a short experience in the dark. One group's game was effectively unplayable because of technical problems, and a reviewer warns it could be difficult for players with visual or mobility issues.
