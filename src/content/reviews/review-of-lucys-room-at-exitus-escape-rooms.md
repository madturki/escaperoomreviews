## What others are saying

Lucy's Room is Exitus's easiest room, aimed at newcomers, kids and small groups. Reviewers generally enjoy it.

Google reviewers say it suits two or three players and is engaging for kids. One mentions an "incredible surprise" inside, and a couple enjoyed it as a date night with the hidden-candy add-on. Reviewers on Morty call the puzzles clever and fun, and one praises staff for letting a nervous child leave and watch the game from the monitors. A Tripadvisor reviewer had a good time there with family.

One Google reviewer found it less challenging than hoped. Another warns it has more locks and puzzles than you might expect from a beginner room: their pair did not finish in time. On Morty, one player found a couple of puzzles hard to read with poor eyesight, and another group lost time chasing a wrong solution and wished they had been nudged with a hint sooner.
