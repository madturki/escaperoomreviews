_Ten years ago, archaeology professor Dr. Jonathan Bancroft mysteriously disappeared. Cleaning out his office, you find his journal: his colleague Professor Milligan vanished inside a newly discovered Egyptian pyramid. Now you and your team stand at the entrance of the ancient, uncharted pyramid, where two professors vanished without a trace._

We have not yet completed a review of Unforeseeable Pyramid at Freeing Canada Station in Richmond Hill. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

An Egyptian pyramid room, billed as the venue's hardest, with generally positive reviews.

Reviewers on Morty praise the immersion, a plot twist on entering the second room, and satisfying puzzles where almost every solve triggers something. The We Try To Escape blog was impressed by realistic, well-made decor and fitting music, complex puzzles tied to the story, a good balance of mechanisms and locks, and a surprising ending.

Morty reviewers mention a minor tech issue, a game that wasn't reset properly, some temptation to brute-force, and times when it was hard to tell whether something was solved. The blog notes it is dark and linear, not suited to young children, and best with at least four players.
