## What others are saying

A Prohibition-era bootlegging room that reviewers enjoy.

Reviewers on Morty say a room that looks simple at first turns out to be full of puzzles, with a cool tactile puzzle, good logic puzzles and well-judged pacing. On Yelp, one group loved it so much they stayed for a second room and praised their in-character actor, and Google reviewers found the puzzles fun and just the right difficulty for a group of five.

One Morty reviewer hit a single puzzle that was too hard for them to solve.
