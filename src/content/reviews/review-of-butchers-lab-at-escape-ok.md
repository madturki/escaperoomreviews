## What others are saying

A dark, spooky lab room with a live actor that draws mixed reviews.

Google reviewers repeatedly call it terrifying, and some enjoyed its scares and outside-the-box thinking.

Reviewers on Morty dislike that only half the group gets flashlights and say the actor doesn't add to the story. Google reviewers complain about ambiguous or illogical clues, no cue when something opens, slow walkie-talkie responses and unhelpful hints. One said the jump scares continued after members of their group had left the room.
