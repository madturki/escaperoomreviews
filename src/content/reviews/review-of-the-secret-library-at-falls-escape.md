## What others are saying

A family-friendly magical library room that reviewers say is large and atmospheric but held back by its puzzles and upkeep.

A reviewer on Morty was pleasantly surprised by the size of the room and loved the atmosphere, noting that the flashlight provided was definitely needed, with mostly lock-based puzzles and some higher-tech ones elsewhere.

The same Morty reviewer called it the most linear room they had ever played, leaving someone in a group of three always idle, and found it hard to know where to go next because some doors opened silently and plenty of irrelevant items were not glued down. A Google reviewer was far harsher, saying the room did not match the website photos, was dusty and dirty with mostly broken props, and had only four puzzles across the first two rooms.
