The owner here is also the room designer, the builder, and the game master. He told us that he built his first iteration in his home country and this is actually his fifth iteration – and it really shows. The attention to detail, high quality props, and insanely good build quality shot this room into our personal favourites as well as our Most Immersive Escape Rooms list.

This room has what we call 360 degree immersion with all walls, ceiling, and floors fitting the theme, near-perfect lighting and audio throughout and also incorporating several other tactile elements. This is the first room we've been in where "sometimes force is needed" and it adds to the realism and excitement of the experience.

The puzzles aren't traditional at all. Though problem solving skills are needed, the puzzles are practical and physical and make use of a lot of cleverly integrated electronics to sell the results.

This room is ideal for around 4 players but is possible for 2 experienced escapists. I think that more than 4 will feel pretty tight. We found it to be a medium difficulty but a really great value.

We think you should book this room today!
