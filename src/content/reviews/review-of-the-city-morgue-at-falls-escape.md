_You were looking for a new doctors office and overhear someone mention a Dr. Boris Keborkian. Suddenly the doctors assistant who led you in quickly locks the door in what you realize looks more like a mortuary. The location you quickly realize is the abandoned city morgue that was condemned decades ago. Your mission is to discover how to escape from here before the doctor returns to start procedures._

We have not yet completed a review of The City Morgue at Falls Escape in Niagara Falls. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A spooky abandoned-morgue room whose atmosphere wins praise, while the game itself gets a more mixed response.

Reviewers on Morty say the room really feels like a morgue and is well decorated, and World of Escapes reviewers call it one of the more realistic escapes they have seen, full of creepy props. Google reviewers loved the props, atmosphere and challenging riddles, found the staff friendly and helpful, and suggest about four players as the ideal group, plus a jacket because the room runs cold.

The most common complaint is pacing: reviewers on Morty and World of Escapes say the game master came in with hints they had not asked for, and several groups finished in about half an hour. World of Escapes reviewers also mention a broken prop, and one group objected to being charged a damage deposit over an exposed wire they felt was a safety hazard.
