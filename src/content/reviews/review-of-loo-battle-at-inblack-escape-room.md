## What others are saying

Loo Battle is a comedic, competitive washroom-themed room where two teams race each other from side-by-side spaces. Opinion is sharply divided between players who love the concept and experienced players who think the execution falls short.

A Google reviewer called it hilarious, with challenging but not impossible puzzles and a unique, entertaining theme. Players on Morty enjoy the head-to-head twist as a fun way to compete against friends, and like the in-room hint button. A few recent reviewers note that staff told them the room had recently been upgraded, and one veteran singled out the final puzzle as a highlight.

The complaints on Morty are consistent: the spaces are tiny and cramped, props are rough or loose (making it hard to tell clues from decor that has come unglued), and the puzzles feel disjointed. Some groups were left unsure what had opened or what to do next, and a couple of reviewers ran into broken elements. Others mention awkward English translations in the bilingual clues, staff walking in unprompted or simply handing over answers, and walkie-talkies that were hard to hear. Several experienced players on Morty also say the venue's glowing online ratings didn't match what they experienced.
