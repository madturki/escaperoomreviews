## What others are saying

Treasure Island appears to be gone along with its venue. World of Escapes lists the room as closed, TripAdvisor reports the Walden Galleria location (later rebranded as All In Adventures Buffalo) as permanently closed, and Morty says the venue is no longer operating. All In Adventures still offers a tabletop Treasure Island game through its Buffalo-area Mobile Mystery program, but that is a portable event format rather than the original room.

We couldn't find any reviews of this room specifically, so here's what people said about the venue in general. TripAdvisor reviewers often praised the friendly, helpful staff, found the puzzles challenging but fair if you used common sense, and liked it as a no-horror outing with friends.

The common complaints were small, plain rooms with posters and footlockers of combination locks, unclear instructions at the start, hard-to-read clues and locks that sometimes wouldn't accept the right code. On Reddit, an experienced Buffalo player advised skipping the venue, calling it too expensive for "a little office" with poorly ordered puzzles.
