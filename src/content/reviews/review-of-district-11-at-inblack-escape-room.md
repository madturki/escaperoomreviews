## What others are saying

District 11 is a 30-minute live-actor horror experience at INBLACK's Eaton location. Reviewers see it as closer to a haunted house than an escape room.

Reviewers on Morty praise the live actor and the fast-paced, high-intensity format, and Google reviewers call it very interactive and fun from start to finish.

The trade-off, according to Morty reviewers, is that the puzzles are easy and some sections send players off on solo tasks, so puzzle fans may find it thin.
