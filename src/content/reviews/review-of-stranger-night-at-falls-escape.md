## What others are saying

A Stranger Things-inspired room about a shuttered 1980s lab, with very mixed reports depending on the condition of the room on the day.

Google reviewers were surprised by how good it was given its campground setting, with one experienced player calling it absolutely worth the price. Another found it well done and interesting, with some puzzles nicely hidden and hard to solve for a good challenge.

That same Google reviewer felt some puzzles were a bit of a stretch, and a reviewer on Morty was badly let down, saying most of the props were broken, which ruined the experience.
