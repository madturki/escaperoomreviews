## What others are saying

A billionaire's-mansion heist room that is generally well liked, including by families.

Reviewers on Morty praise the atmosphere, a mix of classic and unique puzzles that build on each other, a laser element and the host's acted introduction. Google reviewers describe a good story with fun puzzles and a well-judged level of challenge, and one family called it a great family-friendly pick.

Morty reviewers mention a mechanical device that needed staff help, some redundant key-opens-box-with-another-key steps, and a story that fades out near the end. It is linear with limited space, and one group felt 60 minutes was tight.
