I can’t help but start Escape Manor reviews by praising their staff.  They’re always so friendly and are a key part of setting the atmosphere. 

This is a physically large space that, dare I say it, requires at least 4 to complete the puzzles in time. The space is large enough that it can handle up to 6 people comfortably.

There are several unique spaces in this room that each provides its own immersive experience - from the board room to the server room to... well, I can't tell you. The puzzles here are challenging and are largely technology-based and require analytical skills. There are little to no physical puzzles here.  Good spatial awareness and memory will you greatly here!

Don't be discouraged by that description! It is a lot of fun and could make for a great challenge for an work event. We'd give it a difficulty rating of hard.

Despite having attempted this room already, I’d love to give it another shot and see if we can make it the rest of the way through.  It’s a fun challenge.
