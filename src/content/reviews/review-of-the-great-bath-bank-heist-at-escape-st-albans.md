## What others are saying

Escape St. Alban's is a treasure hunt staged inside the historic St. Alban's church in Adolphustown, built around the real 1945 Bath bank robbery. Reviewers describe it as a fun, unusual outing that suits families.

A writer for Naturally Lennox & Addington who played with two teenagers praised the backstory and the church setting. They said hunting for objects that don't quite fit the décor makes you see the building's history in a new way, and that well-timed hints from staff got their first-time group to the treasure just under the hour. Google reviewers call it a challenging hour that was fun and worth the money and time, and singled out the host for praise.

Complaints are scarce, but newcomers should expect the clues to be challenging; the blogger's family relied on staff hints to finish in time. Overall feedback is limited to a small number of reviews.
