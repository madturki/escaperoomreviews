_Dr. Walter Brandt is a high-ranking chemical weapons specialist for the US government. Now foreign powers have recruited him to develop a powerful virus capable of killing their enemies in hours. You and your team of scientists are tasked with investigating him and finding the only known antidote to virus TS-51 before it gets passed into enemy hands._

We have not yet completed a review of Antidote at Escapology in London. We hope to experience this room soon.

## What others are saying

A science-thriller lab room that reviewers consistently describe as an easy, beginner-friendly game.

Reviewers on Morty call it a good first room for beginners and younger players, with a mix of puzzle types that fit the laboratory theme and staff who give prompt hints. The We Try To Escape blog liked the clear, well-detailed story, the variety of fun and sometimes creative puzzles, and the handy whiteboard and background music.

Both sources found the set a bit sparse. Morty reviewers called it barebones by Escapology standards, with one or two elements that felt out of place and a puzzle that relies on telling similar shades of colour apart, while the blog felt the second and third rooms were a little empty and noticed a slight delay in hints.

_Sources: [Morty](https://morty.app/attraction/10637/antidote), [We Try To Escape](https://wetrytoescape.com/antidote-escapology/)_
