## What others are saying

A retro 1980s room built around helping Jay sneak out to a Kiss concert, and one that has sharply divided players.

Google reviewers who enjoyed it praise collaborative puzzles that keep the whole group working together, an iPad hint interface they found unique and easy to use, and a very friendly, helpful host. One noted that the venue's older negative reviews almost scared them off but did not match their visit.

Reviewers on Morty were far less impressed, describing the room as low budget, with a second space that felt unfinished. Both Morty and Google reviewers report broken or missing pieces near the end of the game: a radio clue whose stations would not come through, a missing final prop, and a photograph referenced by the last puzzle that was nowhere to be found, leaving groups to lean on hints or enter the answer from the iPad.
