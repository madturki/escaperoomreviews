Somewhere in the wreckage is a path to an abandoned research station where you can send an SOS and wait for rescue, but only if you can find it fast enough._

## What others are saying

Exitus's newest live room, an alien-planet survival game, is well received by families and puzzle fans alike.

A Tripadvisor reviewer praised the atmosphere and a soundscape that suits the visuals, and Google reviewers describe it as lots of fun, with nice and helpful staff.

The experienced difficulty setting is a real test: both the Tripadvisor reviewer and a Google reviewer who played as a pair needed hints to get out.
