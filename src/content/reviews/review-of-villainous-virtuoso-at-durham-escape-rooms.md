## What others are saying

A magician's-lair room with some real surprises.

A reviewer on Morty enjoyed the surprising, magic-style moments. A TripAdvisor reviewer found it very challenging but entertaining for their grandchildren, with well-judged clues from staff, and Google reviewers describe a fun, challenging family activity.

The Morty reviewer was frustrated by a few props with finicky tech, and one Google reviewer's game was missing a clue, which staff acknowledged.
