## What others are saying

Outbreak is a tech-heavy zombie game set in an abandoned laboratory, where survivors race to send out cure data. Reviewers on Morty and the We Try To Escape blog rate it among the venue's best.

Reviewers on Morty enjoyed the interactive tech, special effects and set design, and say it works for beginners and enthusiasts alike, with just enough fright for people who avoid scary rooms. We Try To Escape was especially impressed by one room-to-room transition and a special effect that made the story believable. They also found the décor almost brand new and called the original, varied puzzles the real highlight.

Some Morty reviewers found that too many puzzles route through a single computer. Another notes one physically demanding area, though it can easily be bypassed. We Try To Escape points out that the game is linear and thinks it is best for groups of two or three; it may be intense for younger kids.
