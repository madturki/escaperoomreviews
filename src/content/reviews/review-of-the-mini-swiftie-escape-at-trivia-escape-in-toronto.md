## What others are saying

This room appears to be retired. The Mini-Swiftie Escape was a limited-time Taylor Swift theme timed to the Eras Tour's stop in Toronto, and the venue's Swiftie Escape ran its final days in December 2024. Trivia Escape's website no longer lists it as a standalone room. The Swift theme lives on as "Swiftie Sessions," one of the categories in its Game Show Escape room. We couldn't find any reviews of the Mini-Swiftie version specifically.

The closest thing we found is the We Try To Escape blog's review of the venue's full-length Swiftie Escape from spring 2024. They enjoyed the music and the many Taylor Swift references, and said non-fans could still solve most of the puzzles. On the downside, they found the decor basic and a bit rough around the edges, the puzzles fairly easy and linear with traditional locks, and felt the room could have used a few more puzzles.

About Trivia Escape in general, Google reviewers often praise the friendly staff and the effort behind this small, budget-friendly business. At least one reviewer felt the puzzles weren't always intuitive and that the overall quality falls below other escape rooms.
