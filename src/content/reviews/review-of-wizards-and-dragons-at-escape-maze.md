## What others are saying

Wizards & Dragons is a wizard-school room at Escape Maze, and players on Morty and Tripadvisor describe it as a polished, well-themed game.

A reviewer on Morty praises the set design's "wow factor", the use of a magic mirror, a whimsical hint system, fair puzzles and good tech, and calls it one of the more accessible rooms they have played. A Tripadvisor reviewer who played with a group of adult friends liked the good variety of puzzles and difficulty, and the attention to detail in the decor.

Complaints are minor. The Morty reviewer wished there were more chances to cast spells, mentions a few narrow areas, and notes the room isn't especially hard.
