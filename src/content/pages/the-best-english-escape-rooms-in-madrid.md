While spending two months in Spain we knew we had to find play as many English-playable escape rooms in Madrid as we could!

For the most part, materials were accurately translated but you will encounter a variance in the amount of English spoken by the game masters. **Learning a new language is tough and it can take a lot of courage to speak with native speakers.** Be patient and just ask them to repeat things if needed.
