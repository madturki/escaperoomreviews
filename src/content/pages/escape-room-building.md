Once the puzzle is right, someone has to make the object. These are the builds, the shops, and the drawing tools.

## Mechanisms without a circuit board

Constructed Adventures shows several [ways to reveal something with water](https://www.constructedadventures.com/how-to-build-a-treasure-hunt/2022/1/7/interesting-ways-to-use-water-in-your-adventures). KJ Magnetics has a [DIY magnetic lock](https://www.kjmagnetics.com/blog.asp?p=diy-magneticlock).

## Sets and fabrication

A fast [globe puzzle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BAET0jWxNXA) is really a tour of fabrication techniques. Rita Orlov's [laser cutting](https://www.getpostcurious.com/post/craft-corner-laser-cutting-pt-2) notes are the shop version of the same idea. James Stanley's [gear puzzle](https://incoherency.co.uk/blog/stories/gears-puzzle.html) is a careful writeup of iterating one mechanical puzzle.

A crowdsourced list of [tools and materials for repair](https://docs.google.com/document/d/14y_AUvQyd2hFUhNVC1c0FAo_luGf-CndL_0Lcz3sucg/edit?usp=sharing) is what people wish they had on the wall of the workshop. Gratuitous Sets posts [set and prop builds](https://www.youtube.com/@GratuitousSets). Van Oaks Props has a large library of [prop-making videos](https://www.youtube.com/@VanOaksProps/videos). [Building immersive environments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A6ooD0EYjNY) is the escape-room-specific talk. Misumi's [inCAD library](https://us.misumi-ec.com/us/incadlibrary/?currentPage=2&isDisplayNew=false) is a repository of mechanical CAD. Lyra Levin's [rapid prototyping resources](https://www.lyralev.in/rapid-resources) cover tools and process when you need a version today.

Electronic hardware is on the [technology](/escape-room-technology/) page.

## Graphics for props

Small paper tools that save an evening:

- A [knob-scale generator](https://github.com/leechwort/knob-scale-generator) for Inkscape.
- Oliver Boorman's generators for [thermometers, dials, clocks, and more](https://www.oliverboorman.biz/projects/tools/examples.php).
- Tonne Software's [meter dial generator](https://www.tonnesoftware.com/meter2.html), in free and paid forms.

## Something you can carry

Not every game needs a room. A photographed [homemade portable escape](https://imgur.com/a/B2wCwRt) and Chris Ramsay [playing a portable escape](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eXY017IWVjE) are the two examples in this collection. The design-process page also points at them, because a portable build is often how a mechanism gets tested.
