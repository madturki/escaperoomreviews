Technology should serve a moment in the room. These are the practical introductions, from mains power through to the small boards that play a sound or move a latch.

## Power and locks

Escape Room Techs starts with [AC wiring](https://blog.escaperoomtechs.com/escapegame/escape-room-wiring-1-of-2-ac) and [DC wiring](https://blog.escaperoomtechs.com/escapegame/escape-room-wiring-2-of-2-dc). Read those before you improvise. EscapeFront's [lock mechanisms](http://thatguywiththepuzzles.com/dlfiles/LockItDownMechanicalEscapeRoomLocks-EscapeFront.html) covers the electric locks people actually install.

[Clean Air Crew](https://cleanaircrew.org/) is the reference for ventilation and watching the air in a closed room full of people.

## Learning the parts

Alastair Aitchison's [tech videos](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCF8H7dYHK6AvJF0EVonO3cw/featured) are the friendly course: clear examples, one idea at a time. Karmisha Jawell's RECON 2021 talk is the [first steps into escape room tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zJed8t7kxO8).

Specific builds worth copying:

- An [inexpensive RFID reader roundup](https://www.patreon.com/posts/rfid-roundup-23115452).
- A [multiplayer jigsaw in the browser](https://roomescapeartist.com/2021/06/06/how-build-multiplayer-jigsaw-web-app-code-lab/), written as a code lab.
- A [tech replacement for directional locks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8daqk-QMRDk).
- How to drive sound from [MP3 player boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8obcTTYtjQM).
- [Logic level shifters](https://electricfiredesign.com/2021/03/12/logic-level-shifters-for-driving-led-strips/) when a 3.3 volt board has to run an LED strip.
- [Soldering LED strips](https://www.triviumgames.com/post/tech-corner-soldering-led-strips), and the [awesome LED list](http://awesomeledlist.com/) of hardware, software, and controllers.
- A [Node-RED MQTT dashboard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gu0Vq2kVNzw).
- A [deep dive on linear actuators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzgFyO_W2nM) and an [introduction to pneumatics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXSjFMs3W6Y).

Physical mechanisms that are not electronic are on the [building](/escape-room-building/) page.
