A theme is a promise about what the hour will ask of people. Some promises should not be made lightly, and some should not be made at all.

Escape Artistry's [themes you should probably avoid](https://escape-artistry.com/problematic-escape-room-themes/) is the direct list, with notes on how designers walk into them.

A few rooms have tried to take a hard subject seriously rather than use it as scenery. The Logic Escapes Me's [review of The Divide](https://thelogicescapesme.com/review/timetrap-escape-rooms-reading-the-divide/) is the account of a room built to talk about homelessness. Creative Time's [The Privilege of Escape](https://creativetime.org/the-privilege-of-escape/) was built to start a conversation about a social issue. Room Escape Artist's [review of a room about September 11](https://roomescapeartist.com/2024/07/20/open-mind-room-escape-11s-review/) is a careful look at one room that took on that day.

If the theme is the problem, the [design principles](/escape-room-design-principles/) page has the companion piece on clichés and the work on cultural bias.
