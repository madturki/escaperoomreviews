We've had the pleasure of visiting and reviewing nearly 150 escape rooms as a couple. Most of those have been in Ontario, but we've also completed many abroad. We've visited almost every venue in Ontario and completed escape rooms across the province with a 72% escape rate.

We know the things that make people talk about your rooms, review them highly, and what makes a room a great experience. Unlike the typical customer, we don't run through rooms just to complete them as quickly as possible. We don't rate them based on the amount of "fun" we had which can be swayed by a wide variety of variables. **We analyze your room across 25 categories we use for scoring** so that you know exactly what needs to be done to be one of the best.

While we give a high level overview for all of the rooms in our reviews, there is much more analysis completed behind the scenes. We diligently comb through 25 areas of analysis and **provide you with feedback on each area** including suggestions that we think are feasible and would provide value. That analysis can help your room go from a once per day booking to the talk of the town, Facebook groups, and other review sites.

We offer this at a variable cost based on **_your_ listed price per person** multiplied by four. This means, you only need to book that room one time because of our evaluation for it to be worth it.

We will also write a complete review for our website and allow you to read this review before posting. You can decide if the review should be posted immediately, or in one month so that you can make adjustments to the room if you'd like to. We will not change our opinion of the room if no changes are going to be made.

Did you know that **all of our public reviews include a "Book Now" link** and that we measure the clicks? **Each year we refer almost 10,000 visitors** to various booking pages each year based on our reviews.
