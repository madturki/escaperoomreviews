We've done our fair share of escape rooms and have our opinion as to what are the most _overdone_ escape rooms. But what does the data say about what rooms are actually out there?

Below is a table based on the research of Scott Nicholson entitled  [Peeking Behind the Locked Door: A Survey of Escape Room Facilities.](http://scottnicholson.com/pubs/erfacwhite.pdf)  The entire pdf is quite interesting, but here, we're going to look at just the data on the room type. We've added a column on the right to show what percentage of the rooms we've done fit into that category.

It's also worth noting that the respondents were made up of 52% Europeans, 25% North Americans, and the rest from around the world.
