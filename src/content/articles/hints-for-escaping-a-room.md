Find the lock, then the solution - don't try to make solutions out of random things on the wall "what could this mean?"

Scott Nicholson from Brantford, Red Bull escape room championship

Listen to the rules.

It's simple enough for average people to do in an hour - you don't need any extra knowledge

Search the room, tell everyone what you found. Put it all in one spot so you can sort it.

When you're done with stuff - locks, keys, clues - put it to the side so you're not distracted by it

Does the "clue" you found fit with the theme? Or is it just a tag from the item manufacturer?

Know your lock types

Know your puzzle types - morse, pigpen, 6 dots = braile, various ways of hiding things (bold, capitals, missing letters)
