## **Are Escape Rooms open in Ontario?**

**The following changes to public health and workplace safety measures are in effect as of March 1, 2022.** For the full list of measures in effect, see O. [Reg. 364/20](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/200364#BK10).

-   capacity limits have been lifted in all indoor public settings
-   proof of vaccination requirements have been lifted
    -   businesses and organizations may choose to require proof of vaccination upon entry
-   other protective measures, such as mask/face covering requirements and passive screening of patrons remain in place
-   businesses and organizations that are open must continue to have [safety plans](https://www.ontario.ca/page/guide-developing-your-covid-19-workplace-safety-plan) in place
