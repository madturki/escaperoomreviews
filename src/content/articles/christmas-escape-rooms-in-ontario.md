Christmas is coming, and Ontario has a string of holiday escape rooms that make a proper road trip. These five are open this season. We have not scored them yet, so this is a booking guide rather than a ranked review.

The route runs from Niagara Falls up through Woodstock, Kitchener, and Barrie, and finishes in Sudbury.

## Niagara Falls: The Last Christmas

![The Last Christmas at Niagara Escapement in Niagara Falls](/images/2026/11/the-last-christmas-niagara-escapement.jpg)

[Niagara Escapement](/category/location/niagara-escapement/) sets The Last Christmas in the Will family manor. William Zenith Will has died, and he is still hosting the annual holiday party. Your group has an hour to work out what his siblings wanted from their secret Santa and get the gifts ready before the party starts. Niagara Escapement suggests 3 to 6 players. The room takes 60 minutes.

## Woodstock: Grinch Escape Room

![Grinch Escape Room at the Woodstock Museum in Woodstock](/images/2026/11/grinch-escape-room-woodstock-museum.jpg)

The [Woodstock Museum National Historic Site](/category/location/woodstock-museum/) is running a Grinch escape room from November 6 to December 19, 2026. The job is to save Christmas and bring some cheer back to Whoville. It is a Friday and Saturday evening game, with one-hour slots from 4 to 9 p.m. Groups of 4 to 6, ages 14 and up, pay $20 a person. A $40 booking fee holds the slot and covers the first two players.

## Kitchener: The Case of the Missing Magical Key

![The Case of the Missing Magical Key at KW Escape in Kitchener](/images/2026/11/missing-magical-key-kw-escape.jpg)

[KW Escape](/category/location/kw-escape/) is hosting The Case of the Missing Magical Key, a North Pole disaster in which a naughty elf has stolen the enchanted toy-bag key. Without it, the presents do not go out. The room was built by a volunteer for the WRHN Foundation, and the proceeds support the dialysis unit at WRHN. It is played at KW Escape, 259 Gage Avenue, Unit 1.

## Barrie: Christmas With the Pranks

![Christmas With the Pranks at Escape Room Barrie](/images/2026/11/christmas-with-the-pranks-escape-room-barrie.jpg)

At [Escape Room Barrie](/category/location/escape-room-barrie/), Noddy the elf has locked away eight of Santa's magical items, and the Naughty and Nice list is stuck with them. You have an hour to get Santa's village working again before he gets back to the workshop. The room is $33 a player for teams of 4 to 10, and it runs 60 minutes.

## Sudbury: Holiday Rush

![Holiday Rush at Puzzled Escape Rooms in Sudbury](/images/2026/11/holiday-rush-puzzled-escape-rooms.jpg)

[Puzzled Escape Rooms](/category/location/puzzled-escape-rooms/) sends you into Christmas Eve, when Santa cannot find the keys to the sleigh. If he does not leave within the hour, the presents do not make it around the world. Holiday Rush takes 3 to 8 players, runs 60 minutes, and costs $30 a player. The puzzles were rebuilt for December 2025.
