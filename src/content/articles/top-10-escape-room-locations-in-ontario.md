These are the ten Ontario venues whose open rooms score the highest on average. A venue's place is the mean of every open room we've reviewed there, so one outstanding room can outrank a longer list. When two averages match, the venue with more reviewed rooms comes first, and a remaining tie goes to the higher top score. Scores on the page are rounded to one decimal; the order uses the exact average.
